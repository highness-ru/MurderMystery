# Privacy Policy — Murder Mystery

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Murder Mystery does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share personal
information.

The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, user
accounts, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

The app stores the user's music preference locally on the device. This
preference is not transmitted to the developer or any third party and is
removed when the app's local data is deleted.

Murder Mystery may contain links to third-party websites for attribution and
licensing information. Those websites operate independently and are subject
to their own privacy policies.

For questions about this privacy policy or the app, please use the support
information available on the Murder Mystery support page.
